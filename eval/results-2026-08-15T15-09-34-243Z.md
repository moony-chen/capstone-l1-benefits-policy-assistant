The auto-enrollment default and pre-tax/Roth contribution choice from POL-007 are **unchanged** by POL-015. (POL-015)

---

**In summary:** Today, you are under the POL-007 terms (4% max match, three-year graded vesting). Starting January 1, 2026, the POL-015 terms take effect (4% max match at a more favorable rate, with immediate vesting).
