### Q01

**Question**: I just got married last month - can I add my spouse to my medical plan now?

**Status**: answered | **Retrieved**: POL-002, POL-003, POL-005, POL-001

Yes, you can add your spouse to your medical plan outside of open enrollment because marriage is a qualifying life event (POL-002). However, there is an important deadline to be aware of:

**You must submit the change within 30 days of your marriage date** (POL-002). Since you were married "last month," you should act immediately and confirm with People Operations whether you are still within that 30-day window, as missing it would mean you'd need to wait until the next open enrollment period (the first two weeks of November, effective January 1) to make the change (POL-002).

If you are still within the 30-day window and proceed, your medical plan options that include spousal coverage are the **Employee+Spouse** tier, available across all three plan options — Bronze HDHP, Silver PPO, and Gold PPO (POL-003).

**One additional note if you are on or considering the Bronze HDHP:** Adding a spouse may affect your HSA contribution level, as the employer seed amount increases from $750 (individual) to $1,500 (family) (POL-005). You'll want to confirm the applicable coverage tier with People Operations.

**Action item:** Contact People Operations right away to confirm you are still within the 30-day qualifying life event window before submitting your enrollment change.

**Citations**: POL-002, POL-003, POL-005

### Q02

**Question**: How much does the company match on my 401k and when does it vest?

**Status**: answered | **Retrieved**: POL-007, POL-015, POL-012, POL-001

There are **two versions** of the 401(k) match and vesting terms depending on the date, as POL-015 supersedes parts of POL-007 effective January 1, 2026.

---

**Today (through December 31, 2025) — POL-007 applies:**

- **Match:** 100% of the first 3% of your contributions, plus 50% of the next 2%, for a **maximum employer match of 4%** of eligible pay (POL-007).
- **Vesting:** The match vests over **three years, one-third per year** (POL-007).

---

**Starting January 1, 2026 — POL-015 supersedes the match and vesting terms:**

- **Match:** 100% of the first **4%** of eligible pay — an improvement from the current 3% threshold (POL-015).
- **Vesting:** Match becomes **immediately vested** upon being credited — replacing the three-year graded schedule (POL-015).

---

**What stays the same regardless of date:**
- Auto-enrollment at 4% after 60 days and the choice between pre-tax or Roth contributions remain unchanged under POL-007, as POL-015 explicitly does not alter those provisions (POL-015).

If you have questions about which vesting schedule applies to **previously credited match amounts**, please clarify with People Operations directly.

**Citations**: POL-015, POL-007

### Q04

**Status**: answered | **Retrieved**: POL-006, POL-005, POL-003, POL-004

No, you cannot have both a Healthcare FSA and an HSA at the same time. POL-006 explicitly states: **"You cannot enroll in a Healthcare FSA and an HSA in the same year."**

For additional context, the HSA is only available when enrolled in the Bronze HDHP plan (POL-005), while the Healthcare FSA allows up to $3,200/year pre-tax (POL-006). You would need to choose one or the other — not both — for a given plan year.

If you have questions about which option is better suited to your situation, please reach out to the People Operations team directly.
